Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are ScoresAI football predictions? +
ScoresAI uses Claude AI trained on millions of football data points — team form, head-to-head records, home/away stats, injuries. Our predictions achieve an average accuracy rate of 68–72% on match outcomes, and up to 78% on selected high-confidence picks.
Is ScoresAI free? +
Yes. ScoresAI is completely free to use. Creating a free account gives you unlimited AI predictions, live scores, and access to the community predictor. No credit card needed.
Which leagues does ScoresAI cover? +
ScoresAI covers over 40 leagues including the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Champions League, MLS, Eredivisie, Liga Portugal and more — with new leagues added regularly.
How do the betting odds work? +
ScoresAI partners with Betway and Bet365 to display live odds alongside every AI prediction. Clicking an odds button redirects you directly to the partner site where you can place your bet. ScoresAI earns a commission — this never affects prediction objectivity.
Can I install ScoresAI on my phone? +
Yes! ScoresAI is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On mobile, tap "Add to Home Screen" from your browser menu to install it like a native app — no App Store download required.
